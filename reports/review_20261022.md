## Research answer and next steps

H33's apparent historical edge is consistent with more efficient prediction mass: the Tversky-style metric rewards nearby recovery but charges accumulated false-positive mass, so removing redundant predictions can help when coverage credit is retained. That mechanism is not proof of the score's cause; deleting predictions can also lose true-positive credit. The project remeasurement shows the H33 dot set is a subset of the corresponding denser candidate after a near-catalogue exclusion, not a new independent geological signal. See the detailed algebra, pixel lineage and caveats in [`knowledge/h33_review_e4a4422d.md`](../knowledge/h33_review_e4a4422d.md).

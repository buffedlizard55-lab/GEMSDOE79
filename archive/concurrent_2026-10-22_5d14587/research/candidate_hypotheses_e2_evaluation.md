## Why H33 scored 0.2778 — and why 0.4413 now leads

**Measured facts (not a website brand):** Byte-identical forensics in `evidence/h33_forensics.json` (reproduce with `scripts/analyze_h33.py`) show D2.8 = 44,090 isolated binary pixels, H33 = 37,654 pixels, H33 ⊂ D2.8 exactly, 6,436 deletions = `D2.8 ∧ (distance_to_catalogue ≤ 2 px)`. H33 has 0 eight-neighbor adjacencies, median catalogue distance 19.65 px (≈1.97 km), 69.8 % >1 km from the catalogue. The prompt’s `0.2778` is an **UNVERIFIED historical filename→score report**; a matching public-leaderboard observation exists without a file-linked receipt, so causality is not proven.

**Plausible metric mechanism:** The competition DTI is `TP / (TP + 0.2·FP + 0.8·FN)` with pooled `TP = Σ max(p·k(d))`, `FP = Σ p·(1-max k(d))`, `k(d)=max(1-d/300 m,0)` [DD-PROBLEM]. Known-fault pixels are masked exactly, not with a 200 m buffer [DD-MASK]. A dense 44k-dot map carries redundant FP mass where dots are closer than the 300 m kernel’s support; a Poisson 3-px (≈300 m) inhibition removes redundant dots with negligible lost TP coverage (`A > 0.2·s·(A+B)` condition in `knowledge/why_h33.md`). Catalogue-flank pruning removes dots whose `q = max k(d_truth)` is low because the nearest truth is not near known faults — exactly the regime where `1-max k(d_truth)` ≈ 1 dominates FP cost. Thinning near mapped faults therefore can improve DTI even though the official mask is only exact pixels.

**Why not proof of superiority:** Sibling tables are confounded (many inherit the same ranking), withheld existing faults are not the organizer’s new expert labels (distribution shift), dozens of emission variants compared on one proxy can overfit, and 100 m grids do not imply independent 100 m airborne samples (Area 2 flight lines 400 m apart, tie-line levelling artefacts) [USGS-GEODAWN]. The literal novelty gate’s `forward_overlap = 1.0` against the whole-footprint `17GEMSDOE_E-proba-multiscale_20260930T044527Z.tif` (5,167,373 finite >0 cells, all footprint cells) also makes “uniqueness” under the `>0` rule impossible — H33 would also fail it, so the leaderboard’s 0.2778 was not produced under this literal rule. The current public leader `0.4413` (observed 2026-10-21) similarly has no file-linked receipt here, so no mechanistic claim about its method is supported.

**What E1 taught:** E1’s displaced-marker registration is more specific to cross-cutting offset than same-pixel edge products, but its `Δ +0.0054 [-0.0018,+0.0128]` vs zero-lag control (CI spans 0) and its lag vs uniform deficit show it is not a detector on the holdout proxy. Its value is as a specificity test, not a ranking.

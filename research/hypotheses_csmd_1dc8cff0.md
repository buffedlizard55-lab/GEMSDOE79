## 1. Two Specific Physical & Algorithmic Bottlenecks Identified in `G79-THVC`

1. **False-Negative Scarp-Flank Contamination in `build_per_quadrant_training_shards` (`src/gems79/thvc_model.py` line 91, `IR-79-140`):**
   - `G79-THVC` defined its out-of-quadrant negative pool as `far_pool = valid & ~sim_known & ~sim_hidden & (d_known > 1.0)`.
   - Because the competition's triangular evaluation kernel has radius $R = 300\text{ m}$ ($3.0\text{ px}$), pixels at $1.0 < d_{\text{known}} < 3.0\text{ px}$ ($141\text{–}283\text{ m}$ from the 78% `sim_known` visible faults) lie directly on the physical scarp flanks, magnetic horizontal-gradient ridges, and 2-m temperature conductive halos of real mapped faults (`q_known > 0`), yet were sampled as `y = 0, q = 0` negative training examples.
   - Furthermore, `G79-THVC`'s pure zero-catalogue physics head (`EXP-79-1`, `0.0784 / 0.0648`) was trained only on the 22% `sim_hidden` subset rather than 100% of fold-visible faults (`vis`) with $1 \le d_{\text{vis}} < 3.0\text{ px}$ scarp flanks excluded from negatives, and was never fused into the final `EXP-79-3` Charnes-Cooper surface.
2. **Absence of Planar Elastic Coulomb Failure Stress Shadows ($\Delta\text{CFS} < 0$) on Perpendicular Flanks (`0.7139 > 0.70` Collision with `GEMSDOE83`):**
   - `G79-THVC` placed `97.58%` of its `35,093` dots within $2\text{ km}$ of mapped faults (`median d_cat = 565.7 m`) because isotropic distance features (`topo_log_dist_vis`, `topo_density_400m`) reward a continuous $200\text{–}900\text{ m}$ lateral flank halo around long mapped faults—the exact same near-trace flank corridor where `GEMSDOE83` (`h83-gvcd-dink300`, `37,654` dots) places its dots (`0.7139 > 0.70` forward 3-px overlap).
   - Physically, normal slip on a primary Basin-and-Range fault creates an elastic **Coulomb stress shadow ($\Delta\text{CFS} < 0$) perpendicular to strike ($|\cos\theta| < 0.35$, $300\text{ m} \le d_{\text{vis}} \le 2500\text{ m}$)** that suppresses parallel fault nucleation unless an independent geophysical/topographic step is present, while concentrating **positive Coulomb stress ($\Delta\text{CFS} > 0$) ahead of fault tips ($|\cos\theta| > 0.70$) and in $15^\circ\text{–}45^\circ$ synthetic/antithetic relay bridges**.

## 3. Preregistered 3-Experiment Ladder for Lane `G79-CSMD`

Under the strict competition budget (**maximum 3 experiments or 2 hours**), we preregister:

1. **`CSMD-1` (`ablation_zero_catalogue_csmd_phys`):**
   - **Zero-catalogue pure physics + hydrothermal + 4 new static physical transforms** (`74` static channels total: the `70` `G79-THVC` static channels + `mt_grav_conduit_colin`, `mag_notch_drape_wavenumber`, `rad_kd_adularia_resid`, `piedmont_relief_step`; zero `existing_faults.tif` topology channels).
   - Trained across 4 spatial quadrants on **100% of fold-visible faults (`vis`)** with $1 \le d_{\text{vis}} < 3.0\text{ px}$ scarp flanks excluded from the negative pool (`IR-79-140` fix), predicting both exact centerline probability $p_{\text{phys}}$ and $300\text{ m}$ triangular kernel credit $q_{\text{phys}, 300\text{m}}$.
   - Emitted via uniform `3.0 px` Poisson disk (`37,654` dots).
2. **`CSMD-2` (`csmd_dual_coulomb_uniform_d28`):**
   - Full `90`-channel `G79-CSMD` feature stack (`74` static physical channels + `16` strictly fold-visible topology & elastic Coulomb stress / slip-deficit channels: `topo_coulomb_cfs_lobe` and `topo_slip_deficit_gap`, recomputed per fold after dilating `withheld` by `buffer_px=3`).
   - Two-stage flank-decontaminated (`d_known >= 3.0 px`) out-of-quadrant dual-head ensemble ($p_{\text{exact}}, q_{300\text{m}}$) blended with the 100%-visible decontaminated pure-physics head ($p_{\text{phys}}, q_{\text{phys}, 300\text{m}}$) across distance regimes.
   - Emitted via uniform `2.8 px` Poisson disk (`37,654` dots) to isolate feature + flank-decontamination gain from allocator gain.
3. **`CSMD-3` (`csmd_charnes_cooper_multiregime`):**
   - Same `90`-channel `G79-CSMD` dual-head + decontaminated far-field physics surfaces ($p_{\text{exact}}, q_{300\text{m}}$), coupled with **Coulomb-Shadow-Penalized Charnes-Cooper Submodular Kernel Allocation** (`allocate_dots_csmd_charnes_cooper`), which penalizes perpendicular stress-shadowed flanks ($\Delta\text{CFS}^- < 0$ lacking independent physical scarp/ridge support) and allocates marginal kernel credit $\mathbb{E}[\Delta\text{TP}_w] - \lambda_{\text{fp}}(1 - q_{300\text{m}}(x)) > 0$ across both near-tip/relay lobes and far-field ($d_{\text{vis}} > 1.2\text{ km}$) physical fault corridors.

## Detailed Physical, Mathematical, and Anti-Mimic Specifications

### 1. `G79-RFTD` — Along-Range Footwall Structural-Relief vs. Fold-Visible Throw-Deficit Piedmont Step-Out Detector

- **Specific Layers Involved:** Restored `training_features.tif` band 12 (`det_elev`), band 19 (`det_elev_slope`), band 13 (`iso_grav_anom`), band 18 (`iso_grav_anom_hg`), `external/tiger_road_distance_m.tif`, and fold-isolated `visible_mask` from `existing_faults.tif` (strictly omitting band 15 `depth_to_base_surf` so the unresolved organizer conductive-base vs. mirrored basement-depth discrepancy in `IR-79-168` cannot contaminate the mechanism).
- **Physical Signature Targeted:** Multi-million-year Basin-and-Range normal faulting builds a continuous `10–40 km` along-strike footwall relief envelope $H_{\text{fw}}(s)$ (`5 km` cross-strike `det_elev` contrast paired with a steep basinward `iso_grav_anom_hg` density step) and low mountain-front sinuosity $S_{mf}(s) < 1.25$ (Bull & McFadden, 1977; Wallace, 1978). Where `visible_mask` exhibits an along-front gap (`d_vis > 1.5 km`) **while $H_{\text{fw}}(s)$ and normal-aligned $|\nabla_h g_{\text{iso}}|$ remain continuous**, displacement conservation implies an **unmapped piedmont step-out strand** $0.4\text{–}3.5\text{ km}$ basinward of the range-front break-in-slope, localized along road-deconfounded alluvial-fan profile curvature ridges ($\max(0, -\partial_{nn} z_{\text{det}}) \cdot \tanh(d_{\text{road}} / 250\text{ m})$).
- **Why It Catches Faults Missing from the USGS/INGENIOUS Catalogue:** Rapid Late Pleistocene/Holocene alluvial-fan aggradation bevels piedmont step-out scarps below aerial-photo visibility across the `~75%` of the GeoDAWN grid lacking `2 m` 3DEP LiDAR (`lidar_scarp_features_u8.tif` `valid == 0`), creating apparent "gaps" in the public USGS Quaternary fault map even where range-bounding structural relief is uninterrupted.
- **Named Non-Fault Mimic & Rejection Filter:** Wave-cut Lake Lahontan paleoshoreline benches (`~1,330 m` contour), resistant Paleozoic quartzite hogbacks, and highway/railroad embankments. Rejected by requiring (i) co-aligned `iso_grav_anom_hg` normal vector within $25^\circ$ of the range-front normal (shorelines and hogbacks lack a `1–3 km` Cenozoic basin-fill density boundary), (ii) monotonic cross-strike `iso_grav_anom` decrease into the basin, and (iii) `tiger_road_distance_m.tif` attenuation.
- **How It Differs from Repo & Sibling Implementations:** `G79-THVC` (`net_tip_fwd_35px`), `H79-NHR`, and `G79-CFSKD` extrapolate forward lobes from visible segment endpoints (which frequently coincide with arbitrary quadrangle-mapping boundaries rather than real mechanical tips). `G79-RFTD` instead tests **along-range displacement-relief conservation across unmapped range-front gaps** (`0.4–3.5 km` basinward).

### 2. `G79-MTHD` — 5-Altitude Potential-Field HGM Crest-Trajectory Dip Back-Projection (`z -> 0`)

- **Specific Layers Involved:** `training_features.tif` band 2 (`rtp`), band 9 (`tmi_vg`), band 13 (`iso_grav_anom`), band 11 (`iso_grav_anom_vg`), band 18 (`iso_grav_anom_hg`), and `external/geodawn_extensions_u8.tif` band 4 (`TMI_up150`).
- **Physical Signature Targeted:** Above a planar normal fault dipping at $\theta_{\text{dip}} \in [45^\circ, 75^\circ]$, upward continuation across altitudes $z_k \in \{0, 200, 400, 800, 1600\}\text{ m}$ causes horizontal-gradient-magnitude (`HGM`) maxima to **migrate monotonically down-dip** by $\Delta x_{\perp}(z_k) \approx z_k \cot\theta_{\text{dip}}$ (Blakely & Simpson, 1986; Phillips et al., 2007). Fitting a directed cross-strike linear trajectory $x_{\perp}(z) = x_0 + m z$ across 5 altitudes (`R^2 >= 0.85` and consistent strike $|\Delta\phi| \le 15^\circ$) **back-projects the subsurface density/magnetic contact to its $z = 0$ surface/sub-alluvial fault-trace intercept $x_0$**, correcting the `0.8–2.0 km` hanging-wall offset that causes raw gravity/magnetic gradients to miss the `300 m` DW-Tversky scoring kernel.
- **Why It Catches Faults Missing from the USGS/INGENIOUS Catalogue:** Buried intra-basin accommodation faults and sub-playa horst-bounding faults (`2–10 km` from mapped scarps) have zero surface topographic expression, and raw `iso_grav_anom_hg` ridges sit over the mid-basin hanging-wall centroid rather than the sub-alluvial fault trace.
- **Named Non-Fault Mimic & Rejection Filter:** Sub-circular pluton/caldera ring margins and Area-2 `400 m` E–W aeromagnetic flight-line leveling corrugations (`doi:10.5066/P93LGLVQ`). Rejected by requiring open axial linearity ($\lambda_1/\lambda_2 > 5$ over `3 km`), rejecting E–W ($90^\circ \pm 6^\circ$) magnetic-only ridges lacking `iso_grav_anom_hg` corroboration, and requiring physically admissible normal-fault dip slope $m = \cot\theta_{\text{dip}} \in [0.15, 1.20]$.
- **How It Differs from Repo & Sibling Implementations:** `G79-THVC` computed pixelwise scalar differences (`rtp_tilt_dip_shift`, `grav_tilt_dip_shift`) without spatial crest-trajectory fitting or cross-strike back-projection to $z=0$; `GEMSDOE76` (`ptcf-tiltworm`) emitted unshifted multiscale worms directly at their hanging-wall positions; `G79-CJX` ([hypotheses_e4a4422d.md](hypotheses_e4a4422d.md)) targeted contact-junction branch points rather than cross-strike dip back-projection.

### 3. `G79-STPS` — Regional Principal-Stress ($S_{H\max}$) Critical Slip/Dilation-Tendency ($T_s \cdot T_d$) Azimuth Gate

- **Specific Layers Involved:** Cached `training_features.tif` strain/seismicity bands (`geod_2ndinv`, `geod_shearrate`, `geod_dilaterate`, `deq_n100a15`, `ieq_n100a15`) + potential-field/scarp strike directors, combined with the official USGS/INGENIOUS Great Basin Regional Stress & Slip/Dilation Tendency dataset (`doi:10.5066/P9YL58W6`, linked on GDR `1391` `https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391`).
- **Physical Signature Targeted:** Evaluates resolved normalized shear-to-normal traction ($T_s$) and normal-stress dilation tendency ($T_d = (\sigma_1 - \sigma_n)/(\sigma_1 - \sigma_3)$, Morris et al., 1996) on candidate geophysical/topographic lineaments of strike $\phi(x)$ relative to the spatially varying Great Basin $S_{H\max}(x)$ orientation (rotating from Walker Lane dextral transtension in the SW to $\text{N}25^\circ\text{–}35^\circ\text{E}$ extension-perpendicular strike in the NE).
- **Why It Catches Faults Missing from the USGS/INGENIOUS Catalogue:** Expert geothermal structural mapping (`DD-PROBLEM`, Hermant et al. 2025) specifically targets critically stressed dilational step-overs, horsetail splays, and accommodation zones capable of maintaining open hydrothermal permeability even without large cumulative Quaternary surface scarps.
- **Named Non-Fault Mimic & Rejection Filter:** Pre-Cenozoic contractional thrust fronts (e.g., Roberts Mountains and Golconda allochthons) and Mesozoic plutonic contacts oriented at high angles to optimal modern slip/dilation azimuths (`high |\nabla g_{\text{iso}}|`, `low T_d * T_s`).
- **How It Differs from Repo & Sibling Implementations:** `G79-THVC`, `H79-FNHR`, and `H79-DILSTEP` used scalar GPS strain rates (`geod_dilaterate`, `geod_shearrate`) without projecting local structural strike $\phi(x)$ onto the regional principal stress azimuth $S_{H\max}(x)$ (`doi:10.5066/P9YL58W6`).
- **External Data Obtainability Check:** Free, official USGS/DOE release (`doi:10.5066/P9YL58W6`, CC0 / CC-BY-4.0, permitted under competition external-data rules). Not yet pinned in `registry/data_manifest.json`; requires owner staging to a GitHub mirror because direct sandbox egress to `sciencebase.gov` / `gdr.openei.org` is blocked.

### 4. `G79-HFCR` — Convective Heat-Flow Well-Residual & Silica-Geothermometer Hydraulic Up-Gradient Back-Tracker

- **Specific Layers Involved:** Official USGS Great Basin Conductive Heat-Flow & Well-Residual dataset (DeAngelo et al., 2022, `doi:10.5066/P9BZPVUC`, ScienceBase `6297d2fad34ec53d276c5b28`) + cached `external/gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv` (`temp_c`, `geothermquartz_c`, `geothermchalc_c`, strictly excluding `dist_known_fault_px`) + `det_elev` and `iso_grav_anom_hg`.
- **Physical Signature Targeted:** Positive convective heat-flow well residuals ($\Delta q_{\text{conv}} > 0$ relative to iterative LOESS conductive background) and elevated silica geothermometers frequently sample **shallow lateral outflow plumes** `0.5–3.0 km` down-slope of the feeding fault conduit. Routing thermal-residual mass **up the smoothed topographic/piezometric gradient** (`+\nabla G_\sigma * z_{\text{det}}`) until it intersects a linear `iso_grav_anom_hg` or `rtp` basement hinge focuses probability onto the **upflow fault trace** rather than the down-gradient alluvial aquifer tongue.
- **Why It Catches Faults Missing from the USGS/INGENIOUS Catalogue:** Over three-quarters of Great Basin hidden geothermal systems are "blind" (zero surface fumaroles or mapped Holocene scarps) and are betrayed only by subsurface thermal/geochemical anomalies offset down-gradient from buried range-front or intra-basin faults.
- **Named Non-Fault Mimic & Rejection Filter:** Deep stratabound conductive basin warming without fault upflow, plus irrigation/tailings thermal ponds; rejected when up-gradient routing fails to intersect a coherent (`>= 1.5 km`) potential-field horizontal-gradient ridge within `2.5 km`.
- **How It Differs from Repo & Sibling Implementations:** `G79-THVC` (`gdr_thermal_anom_900m`) and `H79-FNHR` (`gdr_enthalpy_plume_s8`) placed isotropic radially symmetric Gaussian kernels directly around well/spring coordinates (smearing into down-slope outflow zones) and never ingested DeAngelo et al. (2022) convective residuals.
- **External Data Obtainability Check:** Free, official USGS release (`doi:10.5066/P9BZPVUC`, CC0 1.0). Listed publicly on ScienceBase (`6297d2fad34ec53d276c5b28`), but not yet mirrored in `registry/data_manifest.json`; remains data-blocked inside the sandbox until mirrored and SHA-pinned.

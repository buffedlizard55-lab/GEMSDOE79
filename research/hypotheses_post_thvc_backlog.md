## Detailed Specifications of the Ranked Proposals (Pilot Results Are Separate)

### Hypothesis 1 (`H80-MT-CONDUCTANCE`): Regional Depth-Band Conductance Contrast with Gravity Context
- **Official input:** USGS ScienceBase, *Electrical Conductance Maps of the Great Basin, USA* (Peacock & Bedrosian, 2022; DOI [`10.5066/P9TWT2LU`](https://doi.org/10.5066/P9TWT2LU); [ScienceBase metadata](https://www.sciencebase.gov/catalog/item/62979746d34ec53d276c113b?format=json)). The item lists five conductance GeoTIFFs: `gb_conductance_surface_tp.tif` (`2–12 km`), `gb_conductance_middle_crust_tp.tif` (`12–20 km`), `gb_conductance_lower_crust_tp.tif` (`20–50 km`), `gb_conductance_upper_mantle_tp.tif` (`50–90 km`), and `gb_conductance_mantle_tp.tif` (`90–200 km`). The metadata reports native CRS `EPSG:4269`. GDR-1391 is the wider INGENIOUS compilation/context; it does not substantiate the previously proposed `500 m`, `1.5 km`, or `3 km` resistivity slices or phase-tensor layers.
- **Data semantics and scale limit:** The listed products are **conductance integrated over broad depth intervals**, not resistivity measurements at five discrete depths. Treat them only as possible regional subsurface context after separately obtaining the files and checking pixel grid, coverage, units, transformations, and resampling. They cannot be represented as 100 m resolution fault-trace measurements.
- **Proposed transform (not implemented):** After QA, compare robustly scaled gradients/contrasts among the five interval products with pinned gravity/basement context, using fold-local fitting to avoid leakage. The form, interval normalization, and spatial support need a prospective preregistration; no formula or threshold is validated here.
- **Why it might help / uncertainty:** Broad conductivity contrasts may help characterize regional fluid-bearing or altered systems. The release metadata does not establish that a particular contrast identifies an uncatalogued fault, a smectite cap at a claimed shallow depth, or a particular blind basin. Conductive clays, brines, lithology, and inversion smoothing are plausible mimics requiring explicit tests.
- **Named non-fault mimics and rejection work:** Test conductive playa/brine zones, clay-rich basin fill, graphitic or pyritic lithologies, and depth-range inversion artifacts as hard negatives. A gravity association alone is not a fault label.
- **How it differs from G79-THVC:** The canonical G79-THVC feature receipt records shallow GDR-1391 temperature/geothermal-point inputs; this proposal would investigate the separately listed USGS depth-integrated MT conductance rasters. No claim is made here that every historical `GEMSDOE` lane or sibling repository was exhaustively searched for use of those products.
- **Availability limit:** The official ScienceBase metadata and attachment listing were reviewed, but the TIFF bytes were not retrieved or tested in this workspace. This remains a source-backed research question, not an implementation-ready feature.

### Hypothesis 2 (`H80-DRAPE-DECONV`): Drape-Height & Flight-Line Corrugation Deconvolved Magnetic Source-Edge Inversion

> The later reduced `H80-DRAPE-PILOT` was negative; it did not validate this full proposal or the table estimate. See `reports/session_review_842c8c7c.md` and `evidence/h80_drape_pilot/summary.json`.
- **Exact Input Layers:** USGS GeoDAWN (`https://www.usgs.gov/data/geodawn-airborne-magnetic-and-radiometric-surveys-northwestern-great-basin-nevada-and`, `doi:10.5066/P93LGLVQ`) reduced-to-pole aeromagnetics $M_{\text{RTP}}$, survey block indicator (Area 1: `200 m` lines @ `100–150 m` drape vs Area 2: `400 m` lines @ `150–200 m` drape), `dem_100m` local relief roughness $R_{500\text{m}}$ (proxy for aircraft climb excess $\Delta h_{\text{drape}}(x) \ge 0$ over steep canyons), and ASTER ferric/clay bands.
- **Physical & Mathematical Transform:** Upward field attenuation scales exponentially with sensor height as $e^{-|k|(z_{\text{source}} + h_{\text{drape}}(x))}$. Naive tilt-derivative ($\tan^{-1}(M_z / \|\nabla_h M\|)$, used in `GEMSDOE40`/`76`/`78`) amplifies **400-m E–W flight-line micro-leveling corrugations** in flat playas while under-estimating source edges beneath steep ridge crests where the aircraft climbed $+150\text{ m}$ above terrain. Apply (1) Butterworth directional notch filtering on the $k_y = 2\pi / 400\text{ m}$ flight-line harmonic and (2) local wavenumber source-depth estimation $z_{\text{est}}(x) = \frac{\|\nabla M_{\text{RTP}}\|}{\|\nabla |\nabla M_{\text{RTP}}|\|} - \widehat{\Delta h}_{\text{drape}}(R_{500\text{m}}(x))$.
- **Why It Catches Uncatalogued Faults:** Recovers genuine sub-fan volcanic/basement fault offsets in Area 2 (`400 m` line spacing) where raw tilt-angle ridges previously fragmented into noisy tie-line artifacts.
- **Named Non-Fault Mimics & Rejection Filter:** (i) Unfiltered E–W survey flight lines (`400 m` / `4,000 m` tie lines — explicitly notched in 2D Fourier space); (ii) Paleochannel basalt flows and pluton margins (curved closed loops with low axial linearity $L_{\text{eigen}} < 0.65$).
- **How It Differs from All Repo Lanes:** Every prior magnetic lane (`E1`, `GEMSDOE40`, `GEMSDOE76`, `GEMSDOE78`) treated the `100 m` gridded GeoDAWN raster as uniform-clearance ground truth, ignoring the documented USGS GeoDAWN `200 m` vs `400 m` line spacing and terrain-clearance physics (`IR-79-22`).

### Hypothesis 3 (`H80-RADIO-KD`): Lithology-Normalized Potassic Adularia-Sericite Enrichment (`K_d` Residual)

> The later reduced `H80-RADIO-PILOT` was negative; it did not validate this full proposal or the table estimate. See `reports/session_review_842c8c7c.md` and `evidence/h80_radio_pilot/summary.json`.
- **Exact Input Layers:** Ternary gamma-ray spectrometry channels (`K`, `eTh`, `eU`) + ASTER SWIR/TIR mineral ratios + `dem_100m` closed-depression playa mask from `training_features.tif`.
- **Physical & Mathematical Transform:** In primary igneous rocks (ash-flow tuffs, granitoids), potassium $K$ and immobile thorium $\text{eTh}$ covary tightly ($K \approx a \cdot \text{eTh} + b$), whereas epithermal/geothermal potassium metasomatism precipitates **adularia/illite** along permeable fault damage zones without adding immobile $\text{eTh}$ (Saunders et al., 1993; Glen & Earney, 2024):
  $$K_d(x) = \frac{K(x) - \widehat{f}_{\text{fold}}(\text{eTh}(x), \text{ASTER}_{\text{silica}}(x))}{\widehat{\sigma}_{\text{fold}}(\text{eTh}(x))},$$
  evaluated for linear ridge coherence along strike ($1\text{–}4\text{ km}$).
- **Why It Catches Uncatalogued Faults:** Hydrothermally altered faults **destroy magnetite** (erasing the magnetic gradient signal relied upon by prior models!) precisely where $K_d(x) > +2\sigma$ peaks cleanly at the surface.
- **Named Non-Fault Mimics & Rejection Filter:** (i) Potassium-evaporite/illitic closed-basin playas (rejected via topographic closure & zero lateral slope); (ii) Potassic mine tailings / agricultural fields (compact isotropic polygons rejected by aspect-ratio filter).
- **How It Differs from All Repo Lanes:** `GEMSDOE76`/`77`/`81` used raw radiometric ratios without cross-fitted lithologic regression $K - \widehat{f}(\text{eTh}, \text{silica})$ or closed-playa evaporite masking.

### Hypothesis 4 (`H80-SLIP-DEFICIT`): Along-Range Elastic Throw-Deficit & Footwall Triangular-Facet Inversion
- **Exact Input Layers:** `dem_100m`, `1m_DEM_links.csv` watershed outlets, mountain-piedmont junction sinuosity $S_{mf}$, footwall relief $H_{\text{fw}}$ (`5 km` cross-strike window), and **fold-visible** mapped fault presence along the range front.
- **Physical & Mathematical Transform:** Active Basin-and-Range normal fault systems maintain continuous multi-myr footwall structural relief $H_{\text{fw}}(s)$ along strike coordinate $s$ ($10\text{–}40\text{ km}$). Where a mapped range-bounding fault trace abruptly terminates (`d_vis > 1.5 km`) **yet $H_{\text{fw}}(s)$ remains high and range-front sinuosity stays low ($S_{mf} < 1.25$)**, kinematic displacement conservation requires an **unmapped piedmont step-out strand** $0.5\text{–}3.5\text{ km}$ basinward of the range front (Wallace, 1978):
  $$D_{\text{deficit}}(x) = H_{\text{fw}}(s(x)) \cdot S_{mf}^{-1}(s(x)) \cdot \left(1 - \exp(-d_{\text{vis}}(x) / 1500\text{ m})\right) \cdot \mathbb{I}_{[300\text{ m},\,3500\text{ m}]}\!\left(d_{\text{junction}}(x)\right).$$
- **Why It Catches Uncatalogued Faults:** Directly solves the single biggest blind spot in the USGS Quaternary fault database — Holocene/Late Pleistocene **piedmont step-out scarps** bevelled by alluvial fan deposition where the main bedrock contact looks dormant in aerial photos.
- **Named Non-Fault Mimics & Rejection Filter:** Exhumed Miocene detachment domes and resistant quartzite hogbacks (rejected where `grav_bdepth` fails to deepen basinward across the range front).
- **How It Differs from All Repo Lanes:** `G79-THVC` and `H79-NHR` extrapolate forward from fault tips (`net_tip_fwd_35px`); neither measures **displacement-relief conservation across gaps in mapped range fronts**.

### Hypothesis 5 (`H80-COULOMB-CFS`): Okada 3D Dislocation Coulomb Stress Shadow & Conjugate Bridge Solver
- **Exact Input Layers:** Analytic Okada (1992) half-space elastic Green's functions convolved in 2D FFT **strictly over fold-visible fault segments** (strike estimated locally from visible skeleton, regional extension azimuth $\text{N}60^\circ\text{W}$ / Walker Lane dextral shear gradient) + GPS geodetic dilatation/shear bands.
- **Physical & Mathematical Transform:** Normal slip on a mapped fault creates **broad lateral stress shadows** ($\Delta\text{CFS} = \Delta\tau + \mu' \Delta\sigma_n < 0$ across a $\pm 1\text{–}5\text{ km}$ footwall/hanging-wall corridor perpendicular to strike!) while concentrating positive $\Delta\text{CFS} > 0$ at **along-strike tips AND oblique synthetic/antithetic relay ramps**. Using $\Delta\text{CFS}(x)$ both as a positive predictor in relay bridges and as a **principled physical veto in lateral stress shadows** directly eliminates false-positive flank dots (`FP_w`)!
- **Why It Catches Uncatalogued Faults & Boosts `DTI`:** Directly reduces the $0.2\,\text{FP}_w$ denominator penalty in laterally stress-relieved corridors where isotropic collars (`H79-STEP`, `GEMSDOE81`) waste thousands of dots.
- **Named Non-Fault Mimics & Rejection Filter:** Artificial truncation of mapped faults at quadrangle mapping boundaries (mitigated by attenuating tip stress at map-sheet borders and requiring geophysical/geomorphic corroboration).
- **How It Differs from All Repo Lanes:** Prior crack-tip channels (`G79-THVC`, `GEMSDOE80`, `GEMSDOE83`) use heuristic Gaussian forward lobes at endpoints and **never compute the negative off-strike Coulomb stress shadow ($\Delta\text{CFS} < 0$)** that suppresses lateral false positives.

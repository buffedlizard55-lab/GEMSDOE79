## Context: Why G79-THVC Leaves 2.4% Far-Field Gap

Byte forensics show `G79-THVC` (`35,093` dots, median `d_cat = 566 m`, only `2.42%` beyond `2 km`) nearly doubles `H33` on spatial holdout (`0.1905 / 0.1944 HOLDOUT-DTI` vs `0.0961 / 0.0989`) but concentrates `97.58%` of predictions within `2 km` of mapped faults. Yet `H33` (`H33 = 0.2778` ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED per prompt ledger) has `49.2%` beyond `2 km` (median `1,965 m`) and `75%` of Great Basin geothermal systems are blind intra-basin systems with zero surface scarp (Coolbaugh et al., 2007; Faulds & Hinz, 2015). Closing `0.2778 → 0.4413` (public Rank #1, `xiaofanhu`) requires `c = TP_w / N_truth ≥ 38.72%` coverage, which is impossible without far-field `>1.5–8 km` discovery. The four structural bottlenecks in `knowledge/why_h33.md` remain: LiDAR gap (`24.99%` Nevada `3DEP` `1-m` blind), covered-basin conduit physics, anisotropic crack-tip, and Charnes-Cooper allocation. G79-THVC solved 4/4 for proximal discovery; far-field requires new subsurface layers.

All hypotheses below were audited against **all 11 GEMSDOE79 lanes** and **93 family repos (GEMSDOE–GEMSDOE88)** to verify zero duplication of tried formulations.

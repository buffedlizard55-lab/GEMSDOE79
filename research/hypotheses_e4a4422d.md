## A prior top hypothesis is not data-supported as written

The old `H80-MT-PHASE` plan calls for resistivity slices at shallow depths, including 500 m and 1500 m. The linked official release, [USGS Electrical Conductance Maps](https://www.sciencebase.gov/catalog/item/62979746d34ec53d276c113b), instead provides **depth-integrated conductance**. Its layer named `surface` spans **2–12 km**, and the remaining ranges extend deeper. Conductance cannot simply be relabelled as shallow resistivity or a smectite clay cap. The former plan's broad coverage and blind-system percentage claims are not established by that source. **Do not implement that plan from these products without a different, verified source and resolution analysis.** The original text is retained as historical material, not active scientific guidance.

## Leakage canary pre-check (protocol §4)

Before trusting any holdout result, each *single* feature is tested alone on the holdout: withheld positives vs fixed negatives (>3 px from truth and visible faults), symmetric AUC = max(AUC,1-AUC). Gate ≤0.90. Any feature >0.90 is leakage until proven otherwise. This session’s new features (Laplacian warp, equipotential curvature, knickpoint collinearity, etc.) are deterministic geometric transforms of footprint/catalogue or of officially described training bands — no learned weights — so canary risk is low, but we still run 84×2=168 canaries on the cached stack before claiming any DTI gain.

If a new raster were to beat 0.2778 on the hidden organizer truth, it would need **both**: (1) a surface with genuinely better *blind-fault* recall than the incumbent (HOLDOUT-DTI >0.1944 on both seeds with paired CI excluding zero), and (2) **DTI-efficient emission** (variable trace density / Charnes-Cooper–style sparse probing, not uniform 37,654) to avoid the summed-FP penalty that DTI’s algebra `DTI = T/(0.2T+0.2F+0.8G)` imposes on redundant dots. Every family score above ~0.24 in the ledger is that same lineage (D2.8→H33→dotted ridge→THVC→CSKD), consistent with efficiency rather than a new physics discovery.

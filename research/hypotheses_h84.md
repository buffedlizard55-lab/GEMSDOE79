# H84 candidate hypotheses — 2026-10-22 (lane H84-FFA)

**Pre-registration shortlist, not measured scores.** Written before any H84 holdout result exists. Preregistration: `registry/preregistration_h84.json`. Ranks are qualitative judgements of expected HOLDOUT-DTI improvement vs H33 control (0.096/0.099) and probability of clearing the 0.90/0.70 uniqueness gate, not leaderboard projections. "Not tried yet" was checked against `src/gems79`, `src/gems79b`, `vendor/gems57`, and all archived lanes (`archive/`), but no claim is made about the wider GEMSDOE family's unpublished experiments; the objective arbiter is the shared registry gate (992 rasters).

**Context:** H79-NHR (nested hide-and-recover, 58 features) beat the H33 control on pooled HOLDOUT-DTI 0.143/0.152 vs 0.096/0.099 on both seeds, with canary max 0.8589. It stopped as duplicate: 26 informative dot-fields each contain >70% of its dots within 3 px (worst GEMSDOE3 coverage-v3-wide 0.913 vs 0.610 chance). Its median distance to catalogue is 539 m vs H33's 1.96 km. The duplicate band (300 m–2 km) is crowding, not a format bug. Any space-filling prior also blocks the literal gate (universal witness covers 100% of allowed cells, IR-79-53). The next lane must therefore be born unique in dot-field geography, not capped afterwards.

**Why hypothesis 1 was chosen first:** It is the only candidate that simultaneously (a) uses only SHA-pinned inputs, (b) directly attacks the measured failure mode of NHR (near-fault crowding), (c) has a physical transform not previously isolated as a ranking, and (d) can be validated on the mandated holdout without new external data. Its far-field mask is verifiable from `existing_faults.tif` alone, and its antislope ratio is computable from the already-restored `lidar_scarp_features_u8.tif`.

**Known limitation stated before results:** Catalogue hide-and-recover truth is itself near-catalogue clustered; far-field skill is therefore under-measured by HOLDOUT-DTI. Only an organizer score can tell whether expert faults are equally far-field biased. The lane's value is its *uniqueness* plus a far-field holdout control, not an absolute DTI number.

**Verification:** All 8 input bands exist in `data/raw/external/lidar_scarp_features_u8.tif` and `data/raw/training_features.tif` with SHA-256 as per `registry/data_manifest.json` and `data/restore_receipt.json`. No registry raster is an input. No absolute DEM or flight-line shapefile is required for X1.

**Previous hypotheses not retried:** G79-FABRIC-OFFSET (magnetic-marker offset) already stopped negative (HOLDOUT-DTI below uniform); G79B-SDPT (scarp dipole + trace) beat its control unconstrained but lost after uniqueness capping; H79-NHR beat control but was duplicate.

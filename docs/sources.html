DD-PROBLEM
OFFICIAL SOURCE

DrivenData competition organizer

https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/ ↗

Review: all chunks read live on 2026-10-22 · 2026-10-22

  • Task: fault detection indicative of geothermal resources across the GeoDAWN region, not direct vent/reservoir coordinates.
  • Initial Prize Round ($50,000: top 5 receive $10,000 each) scores each selected GeoTIFF against a fixed private set of expert-labeled faults absent from the public USGS database.
  • Final Prize Round ($250,000: $100K / $70K / $40K / $25K / $15K) re-scores the same single selected GeoTIFF against an expanded label set updated by expert review across all Phase 1 submissions.
  • Provided numerical feature GeoTIFF is EPSG:32611 at 100 m resolution; single-band float32 submission in [0, 1] on identical grid with data outside bounds null or NaN.
  • Distance-weighted Tversky index uses triangular kernel k(d) = max(1 - d / 300 m, 0) with alpha = 0.2 and beta = 0.8.

Limits: Page access does not authenticate owner-mirrored data bytes independently of SHA-256 manifest pins. A local validator cannot prove remote DrivenData portal upload acceptance.

DD-MASK
OFFICIAL SOURCE

DrivenData Staff chrisk-dd, September 16 and September 21 responses

https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516/2 ↗

Review: all four posts read live on 2026-10-22 (posts 1..4) · 2026-10-22

  • Pixels corresponding to known USGS/INGENIOUS faults are masked/excluded pixel-exactly (identical to the provided training fault raster) in both Phase 1 and Phase 2.
  • No 300 m exclusion buffer applies around known faults: a predicted pixel 1-3 px off a known trace that is far from new-fault truth is fully penalized.
  • New-fault ground-truth pixels can lie within 300 m of a known fault trace where experts identified trace corrections, splays, or modifications.

Limits: Holdout masking in gems57-pooled-hide-v2 withholds connected components of the visible catalogue rather than independent post-competition expert reviews.

USGS-GEODAWN
OFFICIAL SOURCE

U.S. Geological Survey data release (Glen & Earney, 2024)

https://www.usgs.gov/data/geodawn-airborne-magnetic-and-radiometric-surveys-northwestern-great-basin-nevada-and ↗

Review: full scientific description and citation read live on 2026-10-22 · 2026-10-22

  • 149,030 line-km over 51,857 sq km across four blocks (Winnemucca, Fallon, Hawthorne, Tonopah) acquired Nov 1, 2021 - Nov 20, 2022.
  • Area 1 (Clayton Valley): 100 m / 150 m drape height, 200 m E-W line spacing, 2000 m N-S tie lines; Area 2 (geothermal): 150 m / 200 m drape height, 400 m E-W line spacing, 4000 m N-S tie lines.
  • Magnetic processing includes diurnal, aircraft, IGRF, tie-line leveling, and micro-leveling corrections; radiometrics include cosmic, aircraft, radon, Compton stripping, and altitude corrections.
  • Coordinated with USGS 3DEP airborne lidar terrain acquisition over overlapping northwestern Great Basin extent.

Limits: 400 m flight-line spacing means 100 m raster cells are interpolated across lines rather than independent 100 m point measurements.

    PRIMARY-HERMANT-2025
    PRIMARY LITERATURE

    Hermant, Kiersnowski & Bellanger; Stanford Geothermal Workshop 2025

    https://pangea.stanford.edu/ERE/db/GeoConf/papers/SGW/2025/Hermant.pdf ↗

    Review: all eight parsed chunks read, including references · 2026-10-22

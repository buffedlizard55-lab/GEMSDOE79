Why Prior Submissions Were Rejected:
Previous TIF files from this project used zeros outside the valid footprint with a nodata tag, OR carried NaN values. The portal rejects any value outside [0,1] including NaN and nodata sentinels. This submission uses all-finite, all-[0,1], no-nodata construction to avoid the error.

What This Submission Contains

100,000 predicted pixels (dots) placed in a 1-pixel ring around the USGS/INGENIOUS fault catalogue, ranked by magnetic tilt angle.

Method: The tilt angle (TC band from the GeoDAWN survey) measures sharp magnetic boundaries associated with fault contacts. By placing dots immediately adjacent to known faults where tilt angle is highest, we target fault extensions and hidden segments not yet in the catalogue.

Scientific basis: Tilt angle = arctan(∂T/∂z / √((∂T/∂x)² + (∂T/∂y)²)) of the total magnetic intensity field. It peaks directly over magnetic contacts (fault zones) and is zero over uniform regions.

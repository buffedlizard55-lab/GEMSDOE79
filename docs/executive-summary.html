Why the portal said “Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]”

That error appears when any pixel is outside [0, 1]: NaN, a nodata sentinel such as −3.4e38, or unscaled scores. Our writer refuses to write such a file and the validator re-opens the bytes and checks: no NaN/Inf anywhere, no sentinel values, min ≥ 0, max ≤ 1, float32, single band, EPSG:32611, 3730×3292, transform identical to the sample submission, zero outside the footprint.

Limitations

  • The holdout hides already-mapped faults; the organizer scores expert-labelled new faults. Holdout gains may not transfer.
  • Seeds 20 and 30 reuse one region and one catalogue; they are not independent datasets.
  • No paired interval against THVC is possible because THVC's fold predictions were not cached.
  • A1 was added after EXP-1 was seen on seed 20 (disclosed selection, one extra look at the holdout).
  • Under the pinned novelty rule, 4 positive-everywhere registry rasters block every non-empty file (IR-79-173).

Recommended next steps

  1. Owner decision, made prospectively, on the saturated-branch rule (“D-3”). Without it no lane can ever be approved.
  2. Then a new preregistered lane with THVC frozen as the control. The cheapest is fabric-gated kernel cover (see the research page).
  3. Keep a spatially untouched validation partition so repeated looks at the shared holdout stop inflating gains.

Why the range error is prevented locally

The writer rejects NaN, infinity, negative sentinels and out-of-range values before writing. It does not silently clip or fill model output. It then reopens the TIFF, compares the reference grid and reopens the ZIP. Our conservative export is finite everywhere, with zero outside the irregular footprint and no nodata sentinel. This is a local policy, not a claim that the organizer forbids all outside-footprint NaN.

Why the family's best file scored 0.2778 (in five sentences)

  1. The 0.2778 file is 37,654 single-pixel dots, all on the support of one lidar/DEM scarp-detector line set (H19-5), 3 px apart, with every dot within 2 px of the mapped catalogue removed (reproduced from the bytes).

  5. This lane builds exactly that: a terrain-only scarp likelihood gated by the line-continuity of its own evidence, emitted with the champion's emitter; 49 % of its dots are outside the H19-5 halo.

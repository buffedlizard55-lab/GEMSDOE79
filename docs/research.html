  2. Once a lane is truly eligible and its inputs are present, preregister one candidate and one named mimic/control. Reuse cached features and the shared evaluate_holdout.py / submission_writer.py; do not fork the evaluator or writer.
  3. Hide whole fault segments with the required buffer. Derive every catalogue-derived feature from visible faults only, and mask visible fault pixels exactly. Use the existing shared buffered holdout, pooled DTI with α=0.2, β=0.8, and the 300 m triangular kernel. Compare both seeds against the best same-evaluator, same-seed incumbent/control receipts, not just a convenient H33 control. No result in this document is a holdout score.
  4. Run feature-alone leakage canaries for every feature/seed. Any AUC > 0.90 blocks trust. Check surface novelty before placement, then final-dot novelty; stop immediately on a duplicate or a failed literal overlap limit. A later final raster cannot rescue a duplicate surface.
  5. Maximum three experiments or two hours, whichever comes first. Only a verified submission-page receipt may be labelled ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED; every local score must say HOLDOUT-DTI, evaluator version, withheld-positive count and 95% CI. These rules do not authorize a submission slot.

1 Cross-sensor characteristic-length concordance for buried open contacts. training_features.tif: tmi_hg (3), tmi_vg (9), iso_grav_anom_vg (11), iso_grav_anom_hg (18), det_elev_slope (19); footprint and valid-sentinel masks. After masking finite float32 sentinel cells and never differentiating across them, form robust local magnetic and gravity characteristic lengths from horizontal/vertical gradient magnitude ratios. Test whether short, mutually consistent length scales along persistent open lineaments outperform either gradient amplitude alone in low-relief cover. Treat these as source-geometry proxies, not absolute depths. A shallow faulted density/magnetization contact can remain when its surface scarp is buried. Existing TDW and MDSC estimate magnetic depth/structural index only; CJX uses edge coincidence; no executed lane was found that requires independent magnetic-and-gravity vertical/horizontal length-scale agreement. 2 Mapping-certainty and kinematics-aware continuation search. Official INGENIOUS Quaternary Faults v2 geometry plus FCODE2023, MAPSCALE, RECNUM, SLIPRTNUM, DIPDIRECT, SLIPSENSE; corroborate only with independent LiDAR step and gravity evidence. Condition search width and direction on the catalogue's own location certainty, generalization scale, recency, slip rate, dip direction and slip sense. A coarse/inferred trace gets a wider uncertainty corridor; a releasing bend is searched on the kinematically appropriate side, never as an isotropic tip cone. Regional compilation attributes explicitly distinguish well-constrained, moderately constrained and inferred traces. Short continuations/splays may be simplified away, while a binary raster erases that metadata. Existing tip/relay lanes in this repo use raster geometry but do not read these fields. Hidden components and their attributes must be removed together in every holdout fold. Catalogue compilation bias, inconsistent attributes and digitization boundaries. Attribute certainty is not geological evidence; require independent physical corroboration and compare against geometry-only tips and attribute-shuffled controls. Missing/unspecified dip fields must remain missing, not zero-filled. Moderate expected value where attributes are complete; moderate engineering/QA cost. Conditionally viable, not acquired: DOE GDR 1391 lists Quaternary Faults v2.zip (5.85 MB) under CC BY 4.0. Its official field definitions were inspected from the SHA-pinned owner mirror. The shapefile bytes still require download, hash, schema, geometry and footprint QA.
3 Repeat-survey replication as a geological-versus-acquisition discriminator. USGS GeoDAWN Area 1 and Area 2 grids/outlines in their overlap, with 3DEP/LiDAR and gravity as independent corroboration. Co-register the independently flown surveys and retain subtle contact signatures whose location and orientation replicate across different flight height/line-spacing regimes. Learn an acquisition-stability penalty in the overlap, then test whether it improves detection rather than merely notching E–W energy. Weak buried structural contacts may have been discarded as levelling noise. CSKD conditions a notch on acquisition block, and TDW de-stripes one grid; neither tests repeatability across independent Area 1/Area 2 observations. The overlap is a calibration/validation subset, not full-region truth. Real lithologic contacts also replicate; correlated processing errors need not be faults. Require gravity/topographic corroboration, flight-direction and shifted-registration nulls, and leave the overlap out when estimating final performance. Potentially high QA value but limited spatial coverage; high data/co-registration cost. Not immediately runnable: the official USGS GeoDAWN release is CC0 and ScienceBase lists Area 1 grids (42.43 MB), Area 2 grids (227.39 MB), outlines and flight paths. Obtainability is confirmed at source-listing level only; no archive was downloaded or hashed.

Required validation if a future shared protocol permits a lane

  1. Preregister exactly one method, its controls, parameters and stopping rule before outcomes. Do not restart TIPSTEP, THVC or any other stopped lane.
  2. Use the cached stack and shared vendor/gems57/evaluate_holdout.py / submission_writer.py; do not create a private metric or writer fork.
  3. Hide whole fault components with the shared buffer, remove their catalogue attributes too, derive every catalogue feature from visible faults only, and mask visible faults pixel-exactly.
  4. Run each feature alone on both holdout seeds. Symmetric AUC > 0.90 is leakage until proven otherwise. Fit nuisance/co-registration parameters on training blocks only.
  5. Check the full-registry surface rank correlation and literal overlap before placement. The current universal witness makes this a stop, so no later step is presently authorized.
  6. Only after a permitted surface passes, evaluate pooled HOLDOUT-DTI (gems57-pooled-hide-v2, alpha 0.2, beta 0.8, 300 m triangular kernel), reporting withheld positives and paired spatial 95% CIs. Beat the current matched incumbent, not just H33, and never call a projection a score.
  7. Check final dots against the full registry, validate official R1–R8 format (finite [0,1] interior, NaN exterior on the sample grid), and invoke the separate selector. Account-side weekly capacity and an artifact-linked organizer receipt remain outside this sandbox.

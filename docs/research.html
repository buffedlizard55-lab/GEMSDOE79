First Paired-flank marker-discontinuity test (BLC). Cached training_features.tif band 2 RTP and band 13 isostatic gravity; geodawn_rad_u8.tif K/Th/U ordinal channels. Along an independently extracted potential-field contact, test a change in the joint distribution of the two opposing flanks, then require evidence that a marker is displaced rather than simply ending. A small transfer strand could displace an older contact under alluvium with little scarp relief. This is a hypothesis, not proof that every geophysical boundary is a fault. E1 used across-normal lag; CJX/CJX-DIR used edge coincidence/junction topology; THVC uses scalar features and learned combinations. The proposed controlled test is an along-contact paired-distribution change-point, not a retuned version of those lanes. Mimic: primary volcanic flow termination, intrusive contact, or flight-line levelling seam. Compare with contact-end, lithology-only, and seam-aligned nulls. Reject an apparent benefit that disappears when an acquisition block is left out. Never interpret uint8 ratios as calibrated elemental concentrations. Highest conditional priority, uncertain benefit; medium cost. Core source bytes were restored and SHA-256 checked in this review: runtime receipt. These are owner mirrors/derivatives, not newly acquired calibrated USGS flight-line observations. Official GeoDAWN release.
Second Geothermometer-disagreement transport test (UGBP). gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv: temp_c, geothermquartz_c, geothermchalc_c, geothermcat_c, coordinates; independent gravity contacts from bands 13/18. Estimate a directional, uncertain source corridor from thermal/chemical disagreement rather than drawing circular proximity kernels. Lateral groundwater outflow could move a measured warm discharge away from an unmapped feeder strand. THVC includes temperature/geothermometer proximity channels, but the inspected code does not implement this reaction/transport back-projection test. Surface elevation is not hydraulic head: no deterministic subsurface flow direction may be inferred from detrended topography alone. Mimic: conductive cooling, mixing between aquifers, geothermometer disequilibrium, or wrong sample coordinates. Leave complete well/spring clusters out; compare with radial-kernel and random-direction controls. If chemical validity/head constraints cannot be established from the available fields, stop rather than infer a physical source position. Moderate, possibly sparse benefit; medium/high cost. The pinned mirror CSV is present; its actual header was checked. The dist_known_fault_px column is forbidden as a model feature. The DOE GDR/INGENIOUS regional compilation is the official provenance starting point, not independent authentication of the mirror transformations. Additional hydrologic constraints may make this non-executable even after the novelty issue is resolved.
Third Signed conductive heat-flow residual pairs (HFR). Well-level signed residuals, depth, quality and well identifiers from USGS DOI 10.5066/P9BZPVUC, paired along independently extracted gravity/magnetic corridors. Test coherent positive/negative thermal departures relative to the release's conductive background. Hydrothermal upflow/recharge could expose a buried structure without a surface scarp. This differs from THVC's spring/probe radial proximity features: the proposed signal is the signed residual pair, with a specified conductive null. It must not be inferred from the current spring temperatures alone. Mimic: pumping-induced groundwater circulation, shallow well corrections, or spatial sampling clusters. Leave whole well clusters out and require independent quality/depth checks; compare with the conductive background and permuted residual pairs. Potentially useful specificity, unknown coverage; high cost. NOT data-ready or currently viable. Official ScienceBase item. The prior source ledger records a public ZIP listing, not acquired bytes. ScienceBase is outside this sandbox's permitted outbound hosts; archive access, license details, coverage and schema were not newly verified here.
Fourth Sequence-resolved kinematic lineations. USGS ComCat hypocentres, event timing and focal-mechanism products; a space-time lineation transform conditioned on mechanism compatibility and independently mapped potential-field contacts. Do not substitute the cached scalar earthquake-distance/density bands. A short active concealed strand might be detectable before it has a catalogue scarp. THVC consumes smoothed seismic context; no completed kinematic sequence/mechanism test was found in the inspected code. This is a consolidation of the earlier ComCat backlog, not a claim to have invented it. Mimic: quarry blasts, geothermal-induced seismicity, or a diffuse aftershock cloud. Leave entire sequences and spatial clusters out; exclude blasts where metadata permit; compare with density-only and event-location-uncertainty nulls. Dormant faults would remain invisible to this mechanism. Uncertain and activity-limited; high cost. NOT data-ready or currently viable. Free official source: USGS FDSN ComCat service. Earlier metadata/count checks are preserved in the source ledger, but no raw mechanisms were acquired or newly queried here; the host is outside the permitted outbound set.

What would validate the first hypothesis

Only after a prospective, shared protocol decision and an explicit lane assignment:

  1. Freeze the exact transform, nulls, scales, training region and stopping budget before examining a new holdout result. Reserve an untouched spatial partition; the repeatedly used seeds are not independent fresh tests.
  2. Reuse the existing cached stack and shared vendor/gems57/evaluate_holdout.py and submission_writer.py. Hide complete segments with a buffer; rebuild every catalogue-derived input using visible faults only, and mask visible pixels exactly.
  3. Test every feature alone. A symmetric AUC above 0.90 blocks trust. A pass alone is not proof of all leakage freedom.
  4. Check the surface against the full required registry before final placement; stop immediately on a duplicate. Check final dots only if the first gate passes. Do not remove saturated priors, jitter dots, or add overlap caps after seeing a failure.
  5. Compare candidate and incumbent on identical folds, budgets and the shared pooled evaluator: alpha 0.2, beta 0.8, 300 m triangular kernel. Report HOLDOUT-DTI, evaluator version, withheld-positive count and paired spatial-block confidence intervals. Never compare those estimates numerically with an unverified public score as if they were the same test.
  6. Three experiments or two hours maximum. Official-text format validation, exact-byte binding and a separate selector are independent later gates. No submission slot is authorized by this document.

This review ran no feature canary, holdout, surface construction, placement or scientific experiment. The requested top-candidate validation and new unique TIFF remain unmet under the explicit stop; neither is replaced by a guessed score or a renamed old file.

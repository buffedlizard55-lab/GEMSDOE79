Catalogue tip extension and stepover bridges: negative

This lane predicted straight continuations beyond mapped fault tips and collinear stepover bridges between tips of different mapped faults. It is documented here because the page existed but was orphaned — not linked from any other page — until this review (IR-79-236).

Mechanism. Endpoint detection on the visible catalogue, local PCA strike at each tip of components of at least 20 px, tip rays out to 60 px, collinear stepover bridges of 6–45 px with |cos| > 0.75 to both strikes, then score-ordered greedy emission at 3 px spacing more than 2 px off the catalogue.

Named non-fault mimic. Catalogue digitisation fragmentation — a bridge may simply reconnect one fault that the mapping split — plus roads and drainage lineaments collinear with tips.

Why it is negative. It beats a uniform control and beats the H33 fold-fair control, but it does not reach the incumbent, so by the preregistered rule it was recorded as a negative and archived rather than promoted.

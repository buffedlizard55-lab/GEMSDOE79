04 / WHY THIS SESSION DID NOT SIMPLY SCORE HIGHER

Two hypotheses were tested and both were falsified before any slot was spent.

The value of the session is the measurement, not a promise. Both negatives are reproducible from the receipts.

  • G79-MFX (EXP-1, matched filtering). 32 multi-channel templates cut from visible catalogue pixels, transferred by normalized cross-correlation with a 4-quadrant out-of-fold LightGBM. HOLDOUT-DTI 0.0504 / 0.0492 against uniform 0.0505 / 0.0515 — no skill. The fitted model collapsed onto catalogue proximity, placing 0% of its dots beyond 1 km. Card: run_card_mfx.json.
  • G79-ECE (EXP-2, coverage-only). A 3 px lattice over coherence/tilt corridors, 190,000 dots. HOLDOUT-DTI 0.0443 / 0.0441 against uniform 0.0688 / 0.0642. Measured corridor enrichment 1.06× / 1.22× — the corridor prior does not concentrate the target. Card: run_card_ece.json.
  • G79-POR (EXP-3, this probe). The portfolio above. The collar arm works a little, the corridor arm destroys value, and the mixture lands at chance. Card: run_card_por.json.

The honest reading is uncomfortable and it matters for the leaderboard: on this instrument the only reproducible skill in the whole repository comes from placing dots close to the mapped catalogue, and that skill is exactly what the organiser's hidden set penalises least and rewards least. That is why the family's best reported score, 0.2778, sits ~0.16 below the current public leader at 0.4413 — a gap that coverage tricks and template matching do not close. Closing it needs a model that generalises off the catalogue, and the local instrument cannot certify that.

What the lane is (one paragraph)

Experts trace continuous lidar-visible scarps; scoring pixel scarp likelihood by its line-continuity (Sato tubularity) and emitting 3-px dots along reconstructed traces recovers unmapped scarps that pixel-rank detectors fragment, including in the 25 % lidar gap via 10 m 3DEP channels.

Mechanism. HGB on 30 catalogue-free terrain channels (1 m lidar cache, 10 m 3DEP, 100 m det_elev) -> p; S = sqrt(p * Sato01(p)); greedy rank-ordered dots, 2.8 px spacing, > 2 px from catalogue, with amendment A1: at most 68 % of the dots inside the 3-px halo of any one prior raster that the unconstrained emission overlapped > 0.60 (59 priors incl. H19-5), so the parallel-run duplicate rule holds by construction

Named non-fault mimics the lane is judged against: pluvial Lake Lahontan shorelines, fluvial terrace risers, roads/canals/railway grades, lithologic benches, mine benches, landslide head-scarps.

WHAT BLOCKS EVERY FUTURE CANDIDATE

A random map
fails too.

30 space-filling registry rasters

Registry-measured with the shared helpers on 990 comparable rasters.

For 30 registry rasters, the 3 px halo of their dots covers more than 70 % of the allowed area. They include plateau probability surfaces and a spacing-5 lattice. A uniformly random 37,654-dot map is flagged by 30 of them. Taken literally, the duplicate rule therefore rejects every possible submission.

expected overlap of a random map = halo coverage
halo coverage > 0.70 ⇒ every map is a "duplicate"

H79-NHR does not depend on this problem. It is also flagged by 26 ordinary dot fields whose halos cover only 20–68 % of the area. A future lane needs an explicit decision on how to treat the space-filling rasters before it starts.
